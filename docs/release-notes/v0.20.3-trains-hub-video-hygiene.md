## Summary

**Trains hub**

- **Month calendar toolbar** — Icon actions for rule, spin, pick, lock, share, history, and eligibility from month view.
- **Score leaderboards** — VS Push and TPIF podiums on the dashboard; reusable leaderboard component.
- **Conductor history & eligibility** — Paginated full-history dialog and eligibility view with score podiums and reset.

**Video uploads**

- **Hygiene coach** — Upload tips based on past review quality; adaptive denser extraction when an uploader×target needs it.
- **Readable job ids** — Support-friendly adjective-noun-hex ids instead of opaque nanoids.
- **Clearer failures** — Classified error messages (retry vs contact platform) on review and queue pages.
- **Chunk reliability** — Long deposit-slip videos requeue OCR chunks via cron instead of self-invoke loops.

**Deposit slips**

- **OCR hardening** — Repair garbled dates from neighbor frames; tolerant deposit-line parsing; block cross-row timestamp zip.
- **Smarter dedupe** — Auto-resolve OCR-variant alliance tags; flagged clusters grouped by reason in review.
